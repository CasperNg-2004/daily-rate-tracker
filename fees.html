Good to know

  • Abroad, card terminal asks "Pay in MYR?" Choosing MYR (dynamic currency conversion) usually adds a big markup. Pick the local currency instead.
  • The real rate is the mid-market rate, the midpoint banks trade at. Every provider adds some margin, so 0% isn't realistic. Use this to compare providers.
  • Ratings are rough guidance, not an official standard. Rates update about once a day, so very small differences (around 0.1–0.3%) can just be the day's movement.
Real rates: Frankfurter API (mid-market reference rates from central banks) and this project's saved history. Everything is calculated in your browser. Nothing you enter is stored or sent anywhere.
